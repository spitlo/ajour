Agustín Ferrando Trenchi became a little obsessed with the moon as a kid. As an adult, he’s taken tons of photos and hundreds of hours of footage of the moon. After seeing an airplane fly close to the moon while he filmed, Trenchi began a quest to capture a plane flying across the face of the moon. Over the course of 10 years, he took photos and videos of birds and statues silhouetted by the moon, but planes eluded him. I realized if I wanted to film this, I was going to have to try a different approach. It wasn’t just going to happen by accident.
